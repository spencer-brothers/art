Brownian Snowflakes: parameter sweep

Data collection · runs · p5.js 1.11.1

1. Design

Each run grows one snowflake by diffusion-limited aggregation: particles enter at radius R, drift inward with a random lateral step, and snap onto a hexagonal lattice next to the first particle they touch. Growth stops when a new particle cannot move. Every combination of the two parameters below is grown once per batch, one run at a time, with seed = 1000 × batch + index, so the full dataset is reproducible.
